# Império Corretora de Seguros — Product Brief

## Identity
**Name:** Império Corretora de Seguros  
**Location:** Arujá, SP — Brasil  
**Type:** B2B/B2C Insurance Broker  
**Founded:** 2015  
**Tagline:** "Segurança hoje, tranquilidade sempre"

## Core Value Proposition
Specialized insurance brokerage offering:
- Corporate health plans (Plano de Saúde PME)
- Group life insurance (Seguro de Vida Coletivo)
- Auto insurance (Seguro Auto)
- Benefits management (Gestão de Benefícios)
- Consortium (Consórcios)

**Key Differentiators:**
- Personalized, humanized service
- 11 years protecting clients
- Partnerships with Brazil's major insurers: Porto, Bradesco, SulAmérica, Allianz, Amil, Unimed
- Rapid turnaround & transparent pricing
- Post-sale support & consultancy

## Brand Palette
- **Primary Dark:** #3d3d3d (cinza chumbo) — trustworthy, professional
- **Primary Darkest:** #111111 (near black) — depth, authority
- **Accent Gold:** #c9a84c (dourado) — premium, elegance, heritage
- **Accent Gold Light:** #e0c06a — highlights, interactions
- **CTA Green:** #1a3d2b (dark green) — calls-to-action, trust
- **Surface Light:** #f2f2f2 (light gray) — headers, backgrounds
- **Text Primary:** #1a1a1a (near black) — body copy
- **Text Muted:** #6a6a6a (medium gray) — secondary text
- **Surface:** #ffffff (white) — cards, containers
- **Border:** #e0e0e0 (light gray) — dividers

## Typography System
- **Display:** DM Serif Display (serif, elegant)
- **Body:** DM Sans (sans-serif, modern, readable)
- **Font Weights:** 400 (normal), 500 (medium), 600 (semibold), 700 (bold)
- **Size Scale:** 0.75rem → 4.5rem

## Audience
1. **Empresários (Owners/HR):** Decision makers, value trust & ROI
2. **Individuals (PF):** Security-conscious, family-focused, price-sensitive
3. **Insurance Professionals:** Compare options, seek transparency

## Key Pages
- **Homepage (index.html):** Hero, stats, service sections, testimonials, CTA
- **Para Empresas:** B2B focus, 4 dedicated product pages
- **Para Você:** B2C focus, 4 dedicated product pages
- **Contato:** Lead generation form, contact channels

## Success Metrics
- Lead generation (forms, WhatsApp CTAs)
- Time on page (content engagement)
- Mobile responsiveness & accessibility
- Conversion funnel completion

## Technical Constraints
- Static HTML + CSS + JS (no frameworks)
- PHP backend CMS editor (editor.js)
- GitHub Pages deployment (gh-pages branch)
- Clean URLs via .htaccess rewriting
- SEO-optimized (Schema.org JSON-LD)

## Design Intent
**Persuade mode:** Convert visitors to leads through trust, clarity, and premium presentation.
- Professional, sophisticated aesthetic
- Clear value hierarchy
- Responsive, accessible, fast
- Emotional trust through design refinement
